# Cutlass Miner — Brand Assets

All assets are strictly monochrome: pure black `#000000` on pure white `#ffffff`
(or inverted). No gray, no gradients, no color. Do not recolor.

## The mark

Angular faceted raven perched on a crescent moon. Geometric low-poly vector
emblem. This is the brand.

release pages, and docs. Keep the mark and text as one unit — do not
re-space, re-font, or break the lockup.

## Rules

- Black on white for light surfaces; inverted for dark surfaces. Never mixed.
- Minimum rendered size: 24px (mark), 20px cap-height (lockup text).
- No drop shadows, outlines, tilts, or effects.
- Clear space around the mark: at least the height of the moon's crescent.
